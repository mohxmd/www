I started this experiment to understand what actually happens after a video is uploaded and before a browser can play it as an adaptive stream.

Across three posts, I follow the project from its first mistaken assumptions to a working local pipeline: FFprobe inspection, FFmpeg transcoding, HLS playlists and segments, background processing, video state, storage, and delivery through Axum.

This is not an attempt to recreate Netflix or YouTube. It is a focused look at the mechanics and architectural boundaries that become visible when one uploaded file has to become a playback-ready video resource.
